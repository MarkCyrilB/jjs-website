About JJ’s

Anti-gutom means anti-hunger. JJ’s Anti-Gutom Cravings is a Korean-inspired food stall in Davao, open since 2026, serving spicy samyang bowls, loaded fries, enoki and seaweed rolls, and ice-cold drinks until 4 AM.

Our mission

To end late-night hunger with bold, spicy, sulit food that is worth the pic, and to make every craving feel like a good time.
